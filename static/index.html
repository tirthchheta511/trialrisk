Will this clinical trial stop early?

About 1 in 7 clinical trials stops early. That wastes years of work and the time of the people who joined. Give TrialRisk a trial and an AI reviewer reads the protocol, stamps a risk, marks the reasons in the margin and pulls up similar past trials.

      Is it any good?

      The model learned from trials that started in 2008-2015 and was tested once on trials that started in 2018-2019. It had never seen them.

      How it was built

        Limits. Trials stop for reasons no protocol shows, such as a pandemic or a company merger. Registry records are written by sponsors and some fields are edited over time. TrialRisk is a research demo, not advice for real trial decisions.

        Data: ClinicalTrials.gov, U.S. National Library of Medicine. Research demo, not advice for real trial decisions.
